# ScreenGate: Market scan

Public desk research, last updated 2 November 2026. It uses vendor pages, trade press and comparison articles, which are often marketing. It doesn't include private demos or contracts.

**Finding: AI review of sanctions alerts already exists commercially.** ScreenGate isn't a new category. It's a prototype showing how this step can be designed, evaluated and piloted responsibly.

## What we found

| Who | Publicly described | Reported claim | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| SymphonyAI | AI agents that match and discount sanctions hits | 99% fewer false positives and 90% less manual effort, in a proof of concept at a US bank (vendor-reported) | [Case study](https://www.symphonyai.com/resources/90-reduction-in-manual-effort-the-power-of-ai-agents-in-sanctions-compliance) |
| WorkFusion | AI agents for sanctions alert review and payment screening | 80% of false positives removed from reviewers; 70% of payment false-positive hits resolved in real time (vendor-reported) | [WorkFusion](https://www.workfusion.com/solutions/streamline-sanctions-screening-alert-review/) |
| Napier AI Advisor | Screening add-on that helps analysts triage alerts | Up to an additional 40% fewer false positives (vendor-reported) | [Fact sheet](https://learn.napier.ai/hubfs/Product_Factsheets/AI_Advisor.pdf) |
| NICE Actimize | Party and payment screening, with AI to reduce false positives | No figure found | [NICE Actimize](https://www.niceactimize.com/anti-money-laundering/sanctions-screening) |
| LSEG | Publishes on sanctions screening software | Not reviewed in detail | [LSEG](https://www.lseg.com/en/insights/risk-intelligence/the-best-sanctions-screening-software-and-companies-in-2026) |

Vendor figures aren't independently verified. We found no independent benchmark comparing these products. A missed true match is a more serious failure than an over-cautious alert, so false-positive claims should be tested on a company's own data.

## Not checked enough

ComplyAdvantage, Dow Jones, FircoSoft, Hawk and Silent Eight appeared in searches or are known names, but we didn't review their products in detail. Bank in-house tools are also unknown.

## What this means for ScreenGate

- **Don't claim it's unique.** Similar capabilities are publicly described.
- **Where the prototype adds value:** it shows a clear, narrow workflow, with human decisions, cited evidence, written limits, tests and a rollback plan.
- **A real company might buy instead of build.** The evaluation and pilot approach here could also be used to assess a vendor.

## Why this matters for a PM

Finding that a product already exists doesn't make the learning useless, but it changes the honest claim. This project is a portfolio prototype, and it doesn't claim a gap in the market.

## What to ask a compliance team

1. What tools review your alerts today, and what do they leave to analysts?
2. How long does review take, and where does quality vary?
3. What evidence do auditors and regulators expect to see?
4. What would you need to trust an AI recommendation?
